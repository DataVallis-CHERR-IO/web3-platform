| ADR-033 | 2026-10-02 | Accepted | **Private files go through the web app, encrypted by the app.** Uploads and downloads of private files (KYB documents now; evidence, invoices and medical documents later) pass through authenticated route handlers, not presigned URLs. | ADR-036 | 2026-10-02 | Accepted | **EUR→USDC target uses the ECB euro reference rate.** At approval the server fetches the latest ECB EUR→USD reference rate (published on working days; the last published rate is used on weekends/holidays), treats 1 USDC = 1 USD, and stores `eur_usd_rate`, `rate_source = "ECB"` and `rate_at` (the ECB rate date). `target_usdc = floor(target_eur_cents × rate × 10^4)` in integer arithmetic (6 decimals). | ADR-040 | 2026-10-03 | Accepted | **Display currency (David, 2026-10-03).** Every visitor can choose the currency amounts are shown in: all currencies of the ECB daily reference rates plus EUR (fiat), and BTC, ETH, POL and USDC (crypto). **Display only:** contracts, the database and all money logic stay in USDC (6 decimals, `bigint`) and EUR targets (ADR-036); a converted amount is marked "≈" and the exact original amount stays visible next to it. Rates: fiat from the ECB file (X per USD = X per EUR ÷ USD per EUR, 1 USDC = 1 USD as in ADR-036); crypto from the **CoinGecko free API** (keyless; an optional free Demo key can be configured later). Rates are fetched only by the server, stored in `app.fx_rates` and refreshed lazily (ECB at most hourly, CoinGecko at most every 5 minutes); a fiat rate older than 7 days or a crypto price older than 1 hour is not used and the original amount is shown instead. **Default** currency from the browser language and region (`Accept-Language` / `navigator.languages`; e.g. `sl-SI` → EUR, `en-GB` → GBP), USD when unknown. The choice is kept in a cookie and, for logged-in users, in their profile (restored at login). Sources are credited on the page (ECB, CoinGecko). | CHERR.IO is global, so amounts must be readable in the visitor's own currency, but the record (on-chain and in the DB) must stay exact and single-currency. Two free sources, no new account. |
