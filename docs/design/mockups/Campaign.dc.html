Surgery for Susan, 7

by Children’s Health Maribor Trust Score 91/100
[Large colour photo: Susan at home with her mother and her drawings — real photo, parental consent on file]

Susan is seven and loves drawing horses. Since spring she has struggled to walk more than a few steps. Doctors at UKC Maribor have scheduled surgery for 28 November, but part of the cost is not covered by insurance.

Children’s Health Maribor has helped families in Štajerska since 2009. They will pay the hospital directly and post every invoice here.

We need €20,000 for the surgery, three weeks of rehabilitation and the travel for her mother to stay with her.

How your money is protected

Your donation waits in a locked account. Children’s Health Maribor receives it in 3 steps. Each next step is paid only after donors approve the invoices for the previous one.

If the campaign doesn’t reach 10% of its goal (€2,000) by 8 October, you choose: get your money back, or send it to the Emergency Pool.
