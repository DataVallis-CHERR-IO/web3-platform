STEP 1

You donate

By card, Apple Pay or crypto wallet. You get an email receipt and a link to follow your money.

STEP 2

The money is locked

It waits in a locked account. Nobody, including us, can send it anywhere except the campaign, back to donors, or the Emergency Pool.

STEP 3

Donors approve, money moves

Charities get the money in 3 steps. Each next step is paid only after donors approve the receipts for the last one.

If a campaign doesn’t reach 10% of its goal, you choose: get your money back, or send it to the Emergency Pool.
