# Being a Cherrion — a guide for the CHERR.IO community

> **Pre-launch.** This guide describes how CHERR.IO is designed to work at launch. Today the platform runs only on a test network with no real money, and Proof of Charity points are not live yet. Rules and numbers may change before launch; the version published at launch is the one that counts.

Last updated: 2026-10-02

---

## What is a Cherrion?

A **Cherrion** is anyone with a CHERR.IO account. You become one the moment you sign up — no verification is needed. Cherrions donate, vote, rate charities and help bring new organisations to the platform. Your activity is recognised with **Proof of Charity** points and levels.

## Getting started

1. **Sign up** with email, Google or a crypto wallet such as MetaMask. If you do not have a wallet, one is created for you automatically.
2. You receive **1,000 points** and reach **Level 1** straight away.
3. Explore live campaigns and the **Charity Market Cap**, then make your first donation.

## What Cherrions can do

| Action | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Donate** | Give USDC to any live campaign or to the Emergency Pool. Minimum 1 USDC. See the donor guide for details. |
| **Vote on milestones** | When a campaign you supported is paid out in steps, vote on the beneficiary's evidence. Your vote counts in proportion to what you gave. |
| **Rate organisations** | After a campaign you supported is completed, rate the organisation from 1 to 5. One rating per campaign. |
| **Vote on the Emergency Pool** | If you gave to the Emergency Pool (directly, or by choosing it for a failed campaign), you can vote on proposals to send pool money to urgent campaigns. |
| **Refer organisations** | Invite a charity you trust. When it passes verification, you earn points. |
| **Start a campaign** | Any Cherrion can raise money for themselves or someone close to them after an identity check. See the fundraiser guide. |

## Proof of Charity points

Points are recorded only for actions CHERR.IO can verify — a donation on the blockchain, a signed vote or a signed rating.

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Complete registration | **1,000** (Level 1) |
| Donate to a live campaign | **100 per 1 USDC** *(provisional — may change before launch)* |
| Vote on a milestone | **200** |
| Rate an organisation after a campaign | **200** |
| Pass the identity check (individuals) | **1,000** |
| An organisation you referred passes verification | **3,000** |

Social-media actions do not earn points at launch.

### Two balances

You have two point balances:

- **Status points** decide your **level**.
- **Reward points** are a running total that never resets.

### Levels

| Level | Status points needed |
|---|---|
| Level 1 | 1,000 |
| Level 2 | 3,000 |
| Level 3 | 6,000 |
| Level 4 | 10,000 |
| Level 5 | 15,000 |

Levels reflect **recent** activity:

- At the start of each month, your Status balance goes back to the minimum of your current level. You keep the level, but you need to stay active to climb.
- If you earn **no points in a month**, you drop **one level**. We will notify you first.
- Your Reward balance is never reset.

### Fair play

- There are daily limits on how many points one account can earn.
- The CHERR.IO team can cancel points earned by abuse, for example fake accounts. Every such change is recorded with a reason.

### What points are — and are not — at launch

Points recognise your contribution and show your level. **At launch, points cannot be exchanged for money or tokens.** A later phase may let Cherrions convert Reward points into the existing **CHR** token. That depends on a legal review and is **not guaranteed**.

## Your voice in milestone votes

Campaigns by individuals, and by organisations with a rating below 4.0, are paid in three equal parts. After each part, the beneficiary uploads evidence: invoices, payment proofs and a short video report.

- You have **24 hours** to vote **yes** or **no**.
- The next part is released if at least **50 %** of the donated amount takes part in the vote and at least **51 %** of the votes say yes.
- If too few donors vote, the CHERR.IO team decides. **Your vote matters** — every vote helps the campaign avoid a manual review.
- If donors vote no, the money not yet paid out goes back to donors.

## The Emergency Pool

The Emergency Pool is shared money for urgent causes. It has a general pool and themed sub-pools, such as medical help, disasters, animals and climate. Money comes from:

- direct donations,
- donors who chose the pool when a campaign failed or was rejected,
- refunds left unclaimed for 180 days.

To use pool money, the CHERR.IO team proposes sending a specific amount to an approved live campaign. The people who contributed to that pool vote for 24 hours, using the same 50 % / 51 % rule. Your vote counts in proportion to what you had contributed **before** the proposal was made, so nobody can buy votes after a proposal appears. The pool can send money only to live campaigns on CHERR.IO — never to any other address.

## The Charity Market Cap

The Charity Market Cap is a public ranking of charities, a bit like a stock market list but for trust. It includes organisations registered on CHERR.IO and organisations imported from public registries in Slovenia, the UK and the US. Each organisation has a public **Trust Score** from 0 to 100, built from:

- donor ratings,
- campaign success rate,
- milestone votes,
- evidence delivered on time,
- verification status.

The formula is published. Organisations that are not yet on CHERR.IO show a **Claim this organisation** button. If you know one, invite it — that is a referral.

## Your data

- On the blockchain, your activity shows only a **wallet address**, never your name or email.
- Your account details are stored privately and protected under GDPR.
- You can delete your account at any time from your account page.
